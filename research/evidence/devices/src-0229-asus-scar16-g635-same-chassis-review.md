## What it proves

The G635 cooling reaches 175W GPU in Turbo at 53.6dB(A), 160W in Performance at 48.8dB(A), and 55W in Quiet at 35.3dB(A). Surface temperatures and palm rests remain controlled. Wi-Fi runtime measured 6h28 at 150 nit and 2h44 at maximum brightness. LatencyMon showed slight limitations for real-time audio.
