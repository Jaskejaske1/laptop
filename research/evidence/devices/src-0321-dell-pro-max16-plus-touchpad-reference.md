# Dell Pro Max clickpad reference — SRC-0321

## Source

https://www.notebookcheck.net/Dell-Pro-Max-16-Plus-review-One-of-a-kind-mobile-workstation.1132122.0.html

## Evidence

Notebookcheck measured the clickpad at 134x90mm and described it as quiet but somewhat soft. Its material is not identified as glass and it is a conventional mechanical clickpad rather than haptic. Notebookcheck's Pro Max 18 Plus review explicitly states that its keyboard and clickpad are identical to this 16-inch model down to their dimensions, so the 18-inch Dell baseline does not gain a larger pad from its larger chassis.
