# MSI Raider 16 Max HX B2WI closure — SRC-0339

- MSI's 2026 launch material, official B2WX specification pages and exact model video-output matrix independently identify **two Thunderbolt 4 ports** for B2WI. The Belgian reseller's TB5 field is erroneous metadata. The prior port conflict is resolved to 2x TB4.
- The 16-inch chassis has a metal lid, plastic keyboard deck and plastic bottom.
- The touchpad is plastic and conventional mechanical. Professional severity varies from smooth-enough tracking to very poor/rattly/inconsistent click feel, but criticism recurs across independent reviews. It remains a material buyer-specific weakness rather than an alleged universal failure.
- The keyboard is generally reviewed positively for travel/crispness and deck rigidity, although the compact three-column numpad/layout is divisive.
