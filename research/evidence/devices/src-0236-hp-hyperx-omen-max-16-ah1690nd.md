# HP HyperX OMEN MAX 16-ah1690nd — SRC-0236

## Source

https://www.hp.com/nl-nl/shop/products/laptops/hyperx-omen-max-16-inch-gaming-laptop-pc-16-ah1690nd-e12dsea-abh

## What it proves

HP lists Core Ultra 7 270HX Plus, 32GB DDR5-5600 (2x16), 1TB Gen5 NVMe, two M.2 slots, two Thunderbolt 4 ports, two USB-A ports, HDMI 2.1, 2.5GbE RJ45 and a glossy 2560x1600 240Hz OLED with 500-nit SDR, 1100-nit HDR and 100% DCI-P3. The live page showed €3799 and nearly sold out when checked.

## Conflict

The same HP page calls the GPU RTX 5070 Ti 12GB in the title/summary but RTX 5070 Laptop GPU 12GB in the detailed graphics line. Official TGP is absent. GPU identity and power therefore remain CONFLICT/UNKNOWN.
