## Evidence

MSI specifies the B3WI RTX 5080 at 125W maximum including Dynamic Boost, 16GB GDDR7, two DDR5 slots up to 128GB, two M.2 Gen4 slots, 2x Thunderbolt 4, 2.5GbE and a 16-inch 240Hz full-P3 OLED.

Tweakers tested the same B3WI chassis with 386H/5080/32GB. It measured 379–384cd/m² white, P3 DeltaEITP 3.15, 9h30 web, 20.3dBA browsing, 33.0dBA PCMark, 52.4dBA Blender and 50.9dBA gaming. It praised the large touchpad, expansion and light-load battery, while criticizing audible browsing fans, keyboard-deck flex and low brightness for the price. It explicitly attributes the Raider performance lead to 175W versus 125W GPU power.

No crash or driver-failure pattern emerged in the professional test. DPC/LatencyMon and long-term firmware duration remain UNKNOWN.
