# PCSpecialist Recoil 16 Hardwareluxx input review — SRC-0322

## Source

https://www.hardwareluxx.de/index.php/artikel/hardware/notebooks/67096-pcspecialist-recoil-16-im-test-vor-allem-mit-wasserkuehlung-richtig-gut.html?start=2

## Evidence

The 123x78mm glass clickpad is slightly left-offset. Hardwareluxx found good glide, precision and speed, crisp though loud mechanical feedback, fast multitouch recognition and effective automatic palm rejection. The reviewer noted a slight loss of precision toward the edges but still placed the inputs among the better mobile devices.
