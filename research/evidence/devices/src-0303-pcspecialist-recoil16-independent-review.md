# PCSpecialist Recoil 16 platform — SRC-0303

- PCSpecialist's Belgian site exposes the Recoil 16 with RTX 5080, 32GB, Mini-LED 1250-nit P3 option, two M.2, 99Wh, RJ45, Thunderbolt 4, multilingual ISO keyboard and a glass touchpad. The French repairability score shown is 7.1/10.
- Hardwareluxx independently tested the AMD 9955HX3D/RTX 5080 version of this Uniwill/AiStone barebone. It found a rigid, well-finished aluminium chassis, firm hinges without annoying wobble and a large 123x78mm glass touchpad with good tracking/click behavior.
- Review thermals were strong: no thermal performance drop, GPU about 75°C, surfaces at most 40.5°C, idle 34.7dBA, gaming 45.3dBA and worst measured 49.1dBA. Battery testing reached 376 minutes at light load and 282 minutes under mixed partial load.
- The 5080 reaches full platform power only in Turbo and especially with the optional external liquid cooler; balanced mode limits it to 150W. This is acceptable evidence handling under the new doctrine, not a wattage rejection.
- The reviewed/current selectable CPU generation is 2025 (275HX or 9955HX3D), so it remains ineligible under the buyer's explicit 2026-model rule. It becomes a high-value chassis/reference target for any genuine 2026 Recoil/Uniwill refresh rather than an active purchase.
