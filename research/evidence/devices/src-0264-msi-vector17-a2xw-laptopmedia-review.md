# LaptopMedia MSI Vector 17 HX AI A2XW — SRC-0264

Source: https://laptopmedia.com/review/msi-vector-17-hx-ai-a2xw-review-the-fastest-gaming-laptop/

LaptopMedia measured the RTX 5090 at 172W average over one hour, 2128MHz, 79C GPU and 75C VRAM. A 30-minute CPU run averaged 4.007GHz, 111W and 87C. Its unit was barely audible at idle and completed 5h42 video playback at 180 nits, which conflicts with Notebookcheck's initial defective/software-corrupted idle state but does not erase it.

The aluminium lid/plastic body was extremely solid without flex or creaking. The 131x81mm trackpad was praised as comfortable, slick and precise. The keyboard was comfortable but its small arrows and numpad were criticized. Two RAM slots, two M.2 slots, replaceable Wi-Fi, screwed battery and accessible fans support long-term servicing. The review's unusually enthusiastic overall verdict should be read alongside Notebookcheck's calibrated noise and stability findings.
