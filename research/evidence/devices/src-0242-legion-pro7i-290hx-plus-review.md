# Legion Pro 7i 290HX Plus review — SRC-0242

## Source

https://www.bestlaptop.deals/articles/intel-core-ultra-9-290hx-plus-tested-arrow-lake-hx-refresh-gets-faster

## Evidence

The 290HX Plus review unit led the tested Windows laptops, improved multithread performance and efficiency over 275HX, and maintained upper-80C sustained CPU temperatures with 104C peaks. Keyboard-deck temperature was about 37C. Highest modes were loud; Balanced was around 49dB while retaining 88% performance and drawing 39% less power. The reviewer notes Legion fan tone is generally not high-pitched.

## Limit

The reviewed GPU was RTX 5090. CPU/platform and chassis behavior transfer as LIKELY, not exact 5080 proof.
