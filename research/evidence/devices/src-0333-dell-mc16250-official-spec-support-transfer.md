# Dell MC16250 official evidence — SRC-0333

Sources:

- https://www.dell.com/support/product-details/nl-be/servicetag/0-eEFndmJ6SFZ1WUFDTXR5RHNNb05qdz090/overview
- https://www.delltechnologies.com/asset/en-th/products/workstations/technical-support/dell-pro-max-16-laptop-spec-sheet.pdf
- https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000125103/ownership-transfer-and-dell-product-registration-guides

Dell's support page resolves the supplied service tag to MC16250. Official specifications confirm 255H, RTX PRO 500 6GB, 64GB CSoDIMM and 1TB options. Crucially, the 400-nit FHD+ panel is specified as 45% NTSC; only QHD+ options are approximately full sRGB. Platform options include two TB4, two USB-A, HDMI, RJ45, replaceable RAM/storage and 64Wh/96Wh battery variants.

Dell explicitly requires ownership transfer for a used system bought through an auction/similar platform. Previous-owner information is essential, proof of purchase may be requested and Dell states 7–10 business days for a domestic transfer. Therefore visible Basic Support expiry is conditional purchase evidence, not proof the buyer can immediately exercise it.
