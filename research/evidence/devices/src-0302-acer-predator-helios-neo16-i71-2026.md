# Acer Predator Helios Neo 16 AI PHN16-I71 — SRC-0302

- Acer Belgium officially presents the new 2026 `PHN16-I71` family with up to Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus, RTX 5080, 16-inch 2560x1600 OLED, 500-nit peak, 100% DCI-P3, AeroBlade cooling, Thunderbolt 4 and Killer Ethernet.
- Acer's own disclaimer says only the top/lid is metal and the bottom is plastic. This prevents assuming workstation construction, but does not prove poor durability.
- Exact current EU transactions exist: Portuguese `PHN16-I71-96ML` at €3599 with 290HX Plus/RTX 5080/32GB/1TB; Dutch `PHN16-I71-956X` at €3999 plus shipping but currently unavailable. Neither regional keyboard may be transferred to Belgium.
- Acer's exact Singapore `PHN16-I71-90CV` proves the full-size I71 chassis can expose RTX 5080 at 175W; treat this as chassis evidence, not proof of the exact EU board mapping.
- Launch material lists two M.2 slots, up to 64GB DDR5-6400 and a roughly 92Wh battery. Exact EU/BE serviceability, touchpad dimensions/material, third-year support price and sustained/noise/DPC behavior remain unproven.
- No professional independent review of the exact 2026 full-size `PHN16-I71` with 290HX Plus/RTX 5080 was found in this pass. Reviews of `PHN16-73` or the thinner `PHN16S-I71` are different chassis/configurations and cannot close the gate.
