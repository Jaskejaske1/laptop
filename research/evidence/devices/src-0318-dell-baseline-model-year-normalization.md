# Dell baseline model-year normalization — SRC-0318

## Sources

- https://www.dell.com/support/product-details/en-us/product/dell-pro-max-mb18250-laptop/resources/manuals
- https://i.dell.com/sites/csdocuments/Product_Docs/fr/fr_dell-pro-max-18-plus-mb18250.pdf

## What they prove

Dell's official support documentation identifies the baseline chassis as `Dell Pro Max 18 Plus MB18250`. Dell's official French repairability sheet for this model is dated 18 July 2025. The exact Belgian `2H3VJ` transaction remains regionally isolated in its existing offer/evidence records.

## Decision relevance

The Dell baseline belongs to the same broad 2025 platform generation as RTX 50 systems such as ASUS G615/G635 and XMG E25. Therefore a blanket 2025 rejection would privilege the baseline inconsistently. Eligibility is based on active sale, RTX generation, remaining support, reviews, firmware maturity and platform quality—not the printed year alone.
