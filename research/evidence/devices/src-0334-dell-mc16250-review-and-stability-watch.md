# Dell MC16250 review and stability evidence — SRC-0334

Sources:

- https://www.rtings.com/laptop/reviews/dell/pro-max-16-2025/
- https://www.reddit.com/r/sysadmin/comments/1s55umu/fleet_of_dell_pro_max_16_mc16250_having_bsod_due/
- https://www.reddit.com/r/Dell/comments/1pcdd98/dell_pro_max_16_randomly_unusable_fans_not/

RTINGS tested an MC16250 with a different CPU/iGPU configuration. It found broad I/O, upgradeable RAM/storage, minimal throttling and about nine hours light use, but a bulky design, loud full-load fans and a washed-out narrow-gamut FHD+ display. Exact RTX PRO 500 thermals, acoustics and battery life remain UNKNOWN.

Recent public reports contain more than a single isolated device: one fleet discussion describes RTX/driver-related BSODs across MC16250 systems and another reports missing-GPU/fan failures across five units. These reports justify a current stability watch but do not by themselves prove every MC16250 or this exact service tag is defective. Do not convert them into a categorical rejection without exact firmware resolution or broader professional evidence.
