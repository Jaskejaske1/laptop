# Razer Blade 16 2026 touchpad — SRC-0329

## Source

https://www.tomshardware.com/laptops/gaming-laptops/razer-blade-16-2026-review

## Evidence

The 2026 pad measures approximately 150x97mm and is one of the largest Windows laptop pads. It retains a conventional mechanical hinge rather than haptics, so click force is less uniform across the surface than on a MacBook-style haptic pad. Same-chassis evidence identifies a glass surface. Earlier 2025 reviews differed on palm rejection, so the size/material are strong while flawless palm rejection is not assumed.
