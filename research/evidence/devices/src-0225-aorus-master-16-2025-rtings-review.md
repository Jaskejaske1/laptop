# AORUS Master 16 review — SRC-0225

## Source

https://www.rtings.com/laptop/reviews/gigabyte/aorus-master-16-2025

## What it proves

The tested Core Ultra 9 275HX/RTX 5080 configuration uses a 175W GPU, replaceable RAM and storage, 240Hz OLED, TB4, TB5, HDMI, microSD and RJ45. Review conclusions report consistent high performance with minimal thermal throttling, but extremely loud load fans, poor light-use battery, plastic construction that feels cheap for the tier, hollow speakers and only 1GbE. Exact Belgian SKU, keyboard and support remain unproven.
