# Dell Latitude E5550 specifications — SRC-0226

https://www.dell.com/support/manuals/en-us/latitude-e5550-laptop/dell_latitude_e5550_bdw/technical-specifications?guid=guid-4c6e19d9-c9a1-4884-95be-57731bbb14c9&lang=en-us

## What it proves

The E5550 family uses fourth- or fifth-generation Intel Core processors and DDR3L. Maximum RAM is 8GB or 16GB depending CPU generation. It provides RJ45, three USB 3.0 ports, HDMI, VGA and optional docking, but no USB-C. Displays range from 200-nit HD to 300-nit FHD anti-glare. Original battery choices were 38Wh/51Wh. Exact local configuration and component health remain unknown.
