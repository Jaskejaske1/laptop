# HP OMEN Max 16 touchpad — SRC-0325

## Source

https://www.notebookcheck.net/HP-Omen-Max-16-laptop-review-Super-fast-but-a-little-bit-too-hot.999164.0.html

## Evidence

The conventional clickpad measures 125x80mm. It is reasonably large and produces a clicky, satisfying response rather than a spongy one. Independent reviews broadly agree that navigation and multitouch are smooth and accurate, but the surface material is not proven as glass and the pad is smaller than SCAR, Strix, Razer and Stealth.
