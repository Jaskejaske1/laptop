# Alienware 16 Area-51 touchpad — SRC-0324

## Source

https://www.notebookcheck.net/Alienware-16-Area-51-laptop-review-Dell-s-best-flagship-gaming-laptop-yet.1027688.0.html

## Evidence

The clickpad measures only 112x65mm. Cursor response and mechanical clicks were responsive/tactile, but multitouch gestures felt cramped. It is one of the smallest pads among the premium 16-inch machines and is a poor match for the buyer's large-touchpad preference despite otherwise strong input hardware.
