# EliteBook 850 G6 review — SRC-0253

## Source

https://laptopmedia.com/review/hp-elitebook-850-g6-review-little-changes-big-improvements/

## Evidence

The Windows review measured 15h50 scripted web browsing and 12h57 video from the 56Wh battery under controlled low-brightness/light-background conditions. Its i5-class unit sustained approximately 2.14GHz after 10–15 minutes CPU load at about 71C and was described as externally comfortable and quiet. It also confirmed PCIe x4 NVMe support. The reviewed AUO panel is not the local LG panel, so panel-specific power and quality cannot be transferred exactly.

These runtimes are upper-bound chassis evidence, not a forecast for Office/OneDrive/Teams/VPN at classroom brightness. They nevertheless support replacing the local 68.7%-health battery when the rest of the machine is retained.
