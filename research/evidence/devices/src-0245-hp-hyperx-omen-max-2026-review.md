# HP HyperX OMEN MAX 16 2026 review — SRC-0245

## Source

https://www.myfixguide.com/hp-hyperx-omen-max-16-ah1000-review/

## Evidence

The tested 2026 Intel chassis ran RTX 5080 at 175W and used two M.2 slots, an 83Wh battery, three fans and vapor-chamber/liquid thermal material. CPU peak reached 104C; the reviewer described fan noise as very high and measured a central upper-surface hotspot near 48.6C.

## Caveat

The source contains some technical-description errors and provides no rigorous low-load acoustic or battery measurement. It is insufficient alone to clear the exact 5070 Ti SKU.
