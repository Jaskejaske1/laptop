# HP EliteBook X G2q family display and I/O — SRC-0214

## Source

https://support.hp.com/us-en/document/ish_14499364-14499440-16

## What it proves

The chassis has two USB4 Type-C ports, one USB-A, HDMI and no integrated RJ45. Qualifying 500/800-nit full-sRGB IPS panels exist at family level, but exact Belgian SKU mapping remains required. The single USB-A and missing RJ45 already fail current A gates.
