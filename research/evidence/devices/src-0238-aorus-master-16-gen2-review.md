# AORUS Master 16 Gen 2 review — SRC-0238

## Source

https://www.techradar.com/computing/gigabyte-aorus-master-16-gen-2-review

## Evidence

The reviewer measured less than 2.5 hours of web use and 3h46 video despite the 99Wh battery. Fans were described as high-pitched at low and high speed and prone to ramp rapidly during light work; Quiet mode did not fully solve this. Cooling sustained high performance, but the low-load acoustic behavior conflicts directly with the buyer's stated priority.

## Transfer limit

The review uses the same current AM6J chassis but an RTX 5090, not exact 5070 Ti SKU `6XJM4EEE64SP`. Chassis acoustics and platform idle behavior are LIKELY, while exact 5070 Ti performance remains UNKNOWN.
