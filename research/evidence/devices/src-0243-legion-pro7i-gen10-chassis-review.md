# Legion Pro 7i Gen 10 chassis review — SRC-0243

## Source

https://www.notebookcheck.net/Lenovo-Legion-Pro-7i-16-Gen-10-laptop-review-400-W-power-supply-with-almost-no-compromises.999828.0.html

## Evidence

Fans stayed at low RPM under undemanding loads without pulsing; idle measured 22.9–26.3dB(A). Performance gaming reached about 54dB, while Balanced reduced noise to about 40dB for a 15% graphics-performance loss. Surface hotspots remained about 40–43C, with cooler palms and gaming keys. The RTX 5080 was full-power and loop-stable. Wi-Fi runtime was 5h23. LatencyMon measured 464.2 microseconds with minor issues and one dropped 4K60 frame. RAM and both M.2 slots are serviceable, although the small third fan must be removed for access.

## Limit

This is the same chassis before the 290HX Plus CPU refresh. Current CPU behavior is separately covered by SRC-0242.
