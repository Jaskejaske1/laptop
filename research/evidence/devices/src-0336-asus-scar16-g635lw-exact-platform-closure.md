## Resolved facts

- The Belgian campaign explicitly includes `ROG Strix SCAR 16 (2025) G635LW`. A new qualifying purchase from 1 June through 31 December 2026 can receive one additional standard-warranty year after registration with invoice by 31 January 2027. The extension changes duration only; software, battery, peripherals, accessories and user-caused damage remain excluded.
- Therefore the current exact Belgian SCAR route has a zero-euro third standard manufacturer-warranty year. It is not onsite service and not Dell ProSupport.
- The exact RTX 5080 review proves a plastic main chassis with only the display lid made of metal. The plastic is described as good quality and keeps the palm rest cooler, but the machine is not an all-metal flagship.
- The touchpad is a large matte-glass mechanical ClickPad with working palm rejection. Reviewers differ mildly on click character: the exact review calls the clicks stiffer/clunkier than the reviewer's older SCAR, while another exact review calls the overall click satisfying. This is normal subjective variation, not a tracking defect pattern.
- Exact G635LW power tables allow 175W GPU in Turbo/Manual and a 230W CPU+GPU cross-load allocation in Turbo. The exact review passed repeated 3DMark stress testing without material degradation. Real games averaged roughly 150–165W GPU because workload/power sharing varies; `175W` is the validated ceiling, not a promise that every frame uses 175W.
- Exact noise evidence: fans can stop or remain below 30dBA in Silent daily use; roughly 40–42dBA Performance, 48dBA Turbo and 52dBA max-fan Manual under sustained loads. No coil whine was heard on the reviewed unit.
- Exact thermal evidence: sustained Turbo gaming was about 85–90C CPU and 80–82C GPU without heat-induced performance loss; palm-rest/keyboard areas stayed cool.
- Exact reviewed battery behavior is approximately four hours of light text work at about 120 nits on the Mini-LED configuration. It is adequate for a desktop replacement, not a long-runtime machine.
