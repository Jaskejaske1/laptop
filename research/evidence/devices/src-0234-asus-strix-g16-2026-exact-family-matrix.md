# ASUS ROG Strix G16 2026 exact family matrix — SRC-0234

## Source

https://rog.asus.com/laptops/rog-strix/rog-strix-g16-2026/spec/

## What it proves

The official 2026 matrix lists Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus with RTX 5080 16GB at 175W, RTX 5070 8GB at 115W, or RTX 5060 8GB at 115W. It does not list RTX 5070 Ti. RTX 5080 configurations include matte 2.5K 240Hz Mini-LED and 2x Thunderbolt 5/2.5GbE mappings; lower GPU configurations use matte 2.5K 300Hz full-P3 IPS and TB4 plus USB-C. All listed branches have two SO-DIMMs, two M.2 slots and a 90Wh battery.

## Notes

This is not proof of Belgian price, stock, keyboard or the coexistence of every family maximum in one Belgian SKU.
