## Evidence

Notebookcheck measured effective cooling, 26.6–26.9dB idle, roughly 51dB gaming, 7h52 Wi-Fi, controlled surfaces and 1626-microsecond LatencyMon with no dropped frames. CPU briefly hit 105C at 215W before stabilizing around 152W/96C; gaming temperatures were substantially lower. TechRadar praised performance/display and over-eight-hour video runtime but criticized build quality. PCWorld confirmed simultaneous 175W GPU and 125W CPU capability, while criticizing inconsistent value/performance and the inputs. HotHardware described the system as cool and quiet with strong I/O and upgrade access. Together these outweigh a single unreplicated owner temperature report for chassis assessment, but they do not yet complete a recurring-pattern firmware/driver audit or price the third support year.
