# Notebookcheck MSI Vector 17 HX AI A2XW — SRC-0263

Source: https://www.notebookcheck.net/The-last-17-inch-Blackwell-Mohican-MSI-Vector-17-HX-AI-gaming-laptop-review.1061610.0.html

Notebookcheck measured a matte 17-inch 2560x1600/240Hz IPS panel at 473 nits average, 99.6% P3, fast response and no PWM, but only 659:1 contrast. Wi-Fi runtime was 6h44 and LatencyMon 1133.8us without dropped 4K60 frames.

The review initially encountered up to 60W idle consumption and excessive noise. BIOS and MSI Center updates did not solve it; reinstalling Windows did. In MSI AI mode sustained CPU output fell by more than 1,000 Cinebench R15 points after two loops; Extreme mode restored stable performance. Cyberpunk measured 60.35dB and 160W GPU in AI/Extreme, 47.4dB/130W in Balanced and 36.21dB/80W in Eco Silent. These are material exact-chassis software/profile and acoustic findings.

The review transfers its case/input assessment from the almost identical current Vector 16 housing: metal lid, solid plastic base, smooth precise 130x80mm touchpad with somewhat flimsy clicks, unconventional keyboard and awkward lower-shell removal.
