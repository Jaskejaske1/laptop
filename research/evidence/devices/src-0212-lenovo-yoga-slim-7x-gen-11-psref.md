# Lenovo Yoga Slim 7x Gen 11 PSREF — SRC-0212

## Source

https://psref.lenovo.com/syspool/Sys/PDF/Yoga/Yoga_Slim_7_14Q8Y11/Yoga_Slim_7_14Q8Y11_Spec.pdf

## What it proves

The family supports 32GB and 1TB, a 70Wh battery and three USB4 Type-C ports. Every display is glossy OLED, onboard Ethernet is absent and there are no USB-A ports. It therefore fails the unchanged Laptop A display and I/O gates regardless of ARM compatibility.
