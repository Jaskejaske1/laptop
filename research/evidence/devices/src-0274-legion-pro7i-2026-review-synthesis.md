The 2026 290HX Plus review reports class-leading gaming and creative performance, excellent cooling, around 5.5 hours office runtime with the dGPU disabled and a strong keyboard. Chassis-transfer reviews of the same 16IAX10H design measure low non-pulsing idle fans at 22.9–26.3dB(A), about 40dB Balanced and 54dB Performance, full-power loop-stable RTX 5080 operation and usable surface temperatures. Battery results span roughly three hours normal mixed use to 4h37–5h23 controlled Wi-Fi/productivity, with almost six hours favourable video playback.

Lenovo officially specifies aluminium top and bottom but a Mylar touchpad. Reviewers broadly praise chassis rigidity and keyboard feel. The touchpad is smooth and mechanically fine, yet not flagship glass/haptic quality; one professional review found it small and too far left. The refreshed chassis therefore feels materially more premium than the MSI Raider 16 Max, but it is not input-perfect.

Firmware history is not spotless. Isolated exact-chassis reports cover S3/BIOS-setting resets, audio noise and earlier BSOD signals; Lenovo issued a July 2026 VBIOS fix for 0x133/0x116 BSODs. This supports active maintenance and acceptance testing, not a claim of Dell-equivalent enterprise stability or a proven recurring failure rate.
