## Findings

| Field | Controlled conclusion |
|---|---|
| GPU | RTX 5080 16GB, official maximum 175W. Exact professional tests show high, stable performance and no thermal throttling; same chassis is a proven full-power design. |
| CPU | 275HX is strong, but Geeknetic found this implementation somewhat power-limited in multicore relative to faster 275HX competitors. |
| Cooling | Vapor chamber/two 158-blade fans; exact 5080 approximately 96C CPU/76C GPU without thermal throttling. Same chassis stays cool externally but reaches 59.75dB(A) at maximum load. |
| Light-load noise | Excellent: fans mostly off at idle and 23dB(A) measured; they settle quickly after load. This must not be confused with the noisier 2026 AM6J Gen 2 behavior. |
| Battery | 99Wh; 2h15 PCMark Office/max brightness, about 4h moderate use, 5h02 mixed Tom's test, 6h44 WLAN same chassis. Protocols differ; none establishes all-day endurance. |
| Display | Official 100% P3/500-nit peak OLED. Profesional Review measured 99.2% P3 and DeltaE 1.01; Notebookcheck measured about 95% Display-P3, 437-nit SDR and 635-nit HDR. Tom's measured 81% P3, an unresolved outlier/method difference retained in the candidate. |
| Touchpad | 133x90mm mechanical pad. Tom's, RTINGS, Geeknetic and Profesional Review report good tracking/clicks; Notebookcheck had repeated stuck-left-click events. `CONFLICT`, likely unit/execution variance, not a universal defect. |
| Construction | Mostly plastic. Structurally usable/stable, but multiple reviewers independently found deck flex, basic fit or a cheap feel for flagship money. |
| Firmware/software | GiMate handles modes and updates. Later updates fixed initial hitch/lag in Notebookcheck's account, but the suite remains less polished; one Tom's benchmark crashed. No multi-source evidence proves chronic driver/platform instability. |
| DPC | Same AM6H/5090 review: 1637.7 microseconds and 10 dropped 4K60 frames. LIKELY chassis risk, not exact BYHC5FRE65SP proof. |
| Repair/support | Two SO-DIMMs, two M.2, battery and fans accessible; bottom RGB cable complicates opening. Repairability 6.1/10. Live seller gives two years; registration adds only three months, so the required third repair year is unresolved. |

## 2025 versus 2026 separation

The Intel AM6H is the better match for the buyer despite its older launch: TB5+TB4, excellent idle fan behavior and a live RTX-5080 route. The 2026 AMD AM6J Gen 2 removes Thunderbolt, and current professional evidence shows intrusive light-load fan behavior and very short battery life. No eligible exact European 2026 RTX-5080/keyboard/support transaction was found, so AM6J cannot silently replace AM6H in the ranking.
