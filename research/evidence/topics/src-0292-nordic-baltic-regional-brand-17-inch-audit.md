## Identity and metadata caution

Multicom category pages contain nearby snippets mentioning Thunderbolt 4/5, but the exact V370W product specification lists two USB 3.1 Type-C ports and does not claim Thunderbolt or USB4. Those category snippets must not be transferred to V370W. The chassis dimensions and V370 naming align with the already documented Clevo/Metabox 17.3-inch platform ceiling; this is a new regional transaction, not evidence of a new 5080-capable chassis.

## Conclusion

Multicom is a meaningful exact regional near-miss because it satisfies P3, RAM, SSD, M.2 and RJ45 cleanly. It nevertheless confirms—not breaks—the 17-inch supply ceiling: full 140W RTX 5070 Ti, older 14900HX and no proven TB4/USB4. No valid full-power RTX 5080 candidate or Belgian/US-International route emerges.
