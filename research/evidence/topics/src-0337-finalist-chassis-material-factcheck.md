# Finalist chassis materials — SRC-0337

| Platform | What is proven | What must not be claimed |
|---|---|---|
| XMG NEO 16 E25 | XMG explicitly specifies aluminium display lid, top case and bottom case; only display frame is plastic. Notebookcheck independently calls it a metal case. | Do not call the plastic bezel a plastic main chassis. |
| ASUS SCAR 16 G635 / Strix G16 G615 | Exact professional reviews prove a plastic main chassis and metal lid. | Neither is an all-metal flagship. Internal reinforcement and good rigidity do not change surface material. |
| MSI Raider 16 Max HX B2W | Metal lid, smooth-plastic keyboard deck and rough-plastic bottom; multiple professional reviews agree. | Do not transfer the aluminium Raider 18/Titan shell to this 16-inch generation. |
| Lenovo Legion Pro 7 16IAX10H | Lenovo PSREF guarantees aluminium top and aluminium bottom. Several reviews call the whole chassis/all keyboard deck aluminium, while exact-generation owner/parts observations call the C-cover/palmrest plastic. | Full-aluminium construction is `CONFLICT`; the safe primary claim is aluminium top and bottom plus a rigid/premium reviewed impression. |
| Dell Pro Max 18 Plus | Professional workstation chassis and MIL-STD/build evidence are positive; construction is robust. | Robustness is not proof that every external part is aluminium, and Dell does not publish every surface material in the inspected exact spec. |
