## Exact-chassis professional behavior

- Notebookcheck tested the current G635LX with 275HX/RTX5090. In combined stress CPU/GPU settled at 50W/175W; no gaming throttling was observed. Cyberpunk noise was 53.6dB(A); idle in Turbo carried 29dB(A), while simple tasks can leave fans off.
- Surface behavior is good for the class: keyboard remained cooler than the rear underside; palm rest/touchpad peaked at 34.3C. The pointing-device score was 86%.
- Wi-Fi runtime was 6h28 at 150cd/m2 but only 2h44 at maximum brightness, reflecting high Mini-LED idle draw.
- LatencyMon measured 2296.5 microseconds and reported slight real-time-audio limitations. Ableton remains outside Laptop B's use case, but this is retained as evidence.
- Exact-family firmware/display-path owner reports remain in `SRC-0354`. G-Helper can reduce Armoury Crate friction but cannot resolve BIOS/EC/external-display defects. Status remains `CONFLICT`, not rejected and not a clean winner.

## Lifecycle interpretation

- The RTX 5090's material seven-year advantage is 24GB VRAM versus 16GB on the 5080; speed uplift at the same 175W ceiling is not the main argument.
- Both M.2 slots are occupied by the factory RAID0 pair. Storage is replaceable, but expansion requires breaking/reconfiguring the array or replacing drives; there is no empty third slot.
- ASUS's current 2026 lineup exposes an 18-inch SCAR 5090, not an official 16-inch SCAR successor. The actively supported 2025 G635LX is therefore the current 16-inch ASUS 5090 route.
