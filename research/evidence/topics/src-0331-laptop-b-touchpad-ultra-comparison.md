## Synthesis

For a buyable full-performance candidate, SCAR currently has the cleanest combination of premium surface, consistently positive professional behavior and no touchpad conflict. Strix wins usable area per euro but likely uses plastic. XMG is proven glass and good, but smaller. Recoil is demoted from presumed premium because exact-current-chassis reviews conflict. Dell is respectable but softer and not larger despite its 18-inch chassis. Raider, Legion, Alienware and AORUS each have a distinct documented input compromise.
