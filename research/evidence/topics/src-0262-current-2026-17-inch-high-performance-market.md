## Evidence

The 17–17.3-inch size is technically an excellent fit for the buyer and reportedly fits the owned Everki Atlas. The current market has nevertheless migrated most full-power RTX 5080/5090 flagships to 16 or 18 inches.

MSI Vector 17 HX AI is the only directly found 17-inch platform with full 175W RTX 5090, QHD+ 240Hz full-P3 IPS, two Thunderbolt 5 ports and a strong HX CPU. Its original datasheet/current family uses Core Ultra 9 275HX and dates to the 2025 generation, so it fails the hard current-model-year rule despite remaining technically attractive. MSI Crosshair 17 HX AI is current but tops out at RTX 5070 8GB/115W and fails the Dell-beating GPU gate.

XMG's current 17-inch Focus/APEX families top out below RTX 5080/5090; the 2026 APEX 17 uses RTX 5070 12GB and a 300-nit 2560x1440 P3 panel, while the Focus 17 ceiling is RTX 5070 Ti. XMG's 2026 high-end RTX 5080/5090 development instead moves to 16- and 18-inch families.

The expanded audit covered the large OEMs (ASUS, Lenovo, Dell, HP, Acer, GIGABYTE, Razer and MSI), current workstation families, and the relevant integrator/barebone routes (XMG, TUXEDO, EUROCOM, PCSpecialist Belgium, CLEVO Europe, SharkGaming, SKIKK, Medion, Origin, Dream Machines, MIFCOM and ONE). It found no second qualifying 17–17.3-inch full-power RTX 5080/5090 platform. The best real non-MSI near-misses stop at RTX 5070 Ti: XMG FOCUS 17, TUXEDO Gemini 17 Intel and SharkGaming 9G17-70Ti. They remain reference records because that GPU class cannot reliably beat Dell `2H3VJ` materially.

This is a market-availability result, not a rejection of the desired size. Keep 17–17.3 inches preferred and immediately revisit if a new 2026 full-power 5080/5090 family appears.
