### Razer Blade 16 2026 — current reject

- Razer Europe currently lists RTX 5080/64GB/1TB US, French and German layouts at €5299.99.
- The 386H is now correctly eligible under the buyer's Panther Lake exception; CPU naming is no longer the rejection.
- Fixed LPDDR5X and price remain lifecycle/value penalties. Razer's own RazerCare FAQ says plans are currently available only in the US, Canada, Singapore and Hong Kong, so no proven Belgian three-year route exists.
- Exact reviews prove premium aluminium construction, large touchpad, TB5, strong balanced behavior and roughly 12h46 web runtime. These qualities do not cure price/support.

### ASUS Zephyrus G16 2026 — current reject

- Exact Belgian `GU606AW-TB009W` remains €4749 before a third year, 32GB soldered and 160W maximum RTX 5080.
- Its 386H is eligible, and missing RJ45 is no longer a hard failure. Price, fixed 32GB and lower sustained workstation headroom remain sufficient rejection reasons.
- Professional review evidence confirms exceptional battery, full-metal build and large premium touchpad.

### Other audited no-RJ45 layers — no finalist

- Razer Blade 16 2025 clearance: exact 64GB/2TB/RTX5080 German configuration was €3799.99 but unavailable and physically QWERTZ; no eligible BE/US route plus three-year support was found.
- Acer Predator Triton 16 AI: thin 2025 H-class platform with fixed memory/lower GPU-power behavior; no exact eligible Belgian transaction beating the current shortlist.
- ThinkPad P1 Gen 8 and Dell Pro Max 16 Premium: workstation-grade support/inputs are attractive, but their thin RTX Pro implementations do not establish a material GPU-performance win over the Dell `2H3VJ` baseline; configurable/family starting prices cannot be mapped to top graphics.
- ZBook Studio current Belgian evidence remains older Ada-generation or substantially over budget.
- Dell XPS 16, Samsung Galaxy Book Ultra, Lenovo Yoga Pro 9i, Gigabyte AERO and similar creator lines stop below the required GPU/VRAM/performance class.

## Conclusion

Relaxing integrated RJ45 expands the premium/mobile choice but does not produce a current winner. ProArt P16 RTX 5090 becomes the only meaningful new deal watch. Blade and Zephyrus become technically eligible on Ethernet/CPU interpretation yet remain decisively blocked by price, support and fixed memory. MSI Stealth 16 AI+ is not part of the no-RJ45 reopening because the exact chassis already has integrated 2.5GbE; MSI's 175W marketing line refers to a configuration/platform power claim, while exact B3WI review evidence still establishes a 125W RTX 5080 implementation.
