# Finalist public-evidence closure — SRC-0340

The public manufacturer, support, retailer and professional-review layers were rechecked for every unresolved field in the active six-device comparison. `UNKNOWN` now means the datum is not publicly exposed for the exact unit, not that it was skipped.

## Closed in this pass

- SCAR: exact three-year standard-warranty route, plastic-main/metal-lid construction, exact RTX 5080 sustained behavior, fan/noise/thermal and battery behavior.
- XMG: aluminium lid/top/bottom with plastic bezel; official exact 175W sustained GPU capability and 240W combined air-cooling capability.
- Legion: primary material wording and current BIOS state; no newer corrective BIOS exists.
- MSI: B2WI has 2x TB4, not TB5; plastic touchpad/deck/base evidence is recurring and no longer merely presumed.
- Strix: two M.2 slots and factory Gen4 storage are proven; Gen5 compatibility is not required and is not credited.

## Irreducible exact-unit unknowns

- Dell `2H3VJ`: Dell publishes the RTX PRO 3000 identity/VRAM but not its exact configured TGP. A secondary database says 115W, which is insufficient to upgrade the field from `UNKNOWN`. No professional review of exact CPU/GPU SKU provides exact acoustics, sustained trace or DPC.
- SCAR G635LW RTX 5080 and XMG E25 RTX 5080: no controlled exact-unit professional LatencyMon result was found. Same-chassis values and owner incidents cannot be relabelled exact proof.
- Lenovo: only Lenovo can close Q7CN78WW with a superseding firmware package or formal engineering statement; absence of a fix is now verified.
- Five-to-seven-year firmware, replacement-panel and OLED/battery life cannot be measured in 2026 for a 2025/2026 product. Warranty duration, current parts, replaceability and update history are evidence; future availability is inherently not provable.
- Exact internal panel, speaker, NIC or SSD supplier can vary by production batch unless the exact delivered unit's hardware IDs/BOM are read. These fields are not legitimate pre-purchase blockers when the guaranteed performance specification is already proven.

No recommendation may convert these limits into guessed facts. They should be handled as explicit residual risk, not as an endless search queue.
