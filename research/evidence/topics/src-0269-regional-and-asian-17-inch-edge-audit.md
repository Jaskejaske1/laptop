## Confirmed regional additions

- TERRA currently lists a 17.3-inch Gamer Elite 5v4 with Core Ultra 9 275HX, 32GB/2TB and RTX 5070 8GB. It is a real additional European 17-inch family, but well below the GPU/VRAM floor.
- HP's Belgian and German current catalogs show OMEN 17-db branches with RTX 5060/5070, while the RTX 5080 is attached to OMEN MAX 16. A South-African article's “OMEN 17 RTX 5080” combination could not be mapped to an exact HP part number and conflicts with HP's catalogs; it is not product evidence.

## Chinese-market claims requiring primary proof

Chinese search results claim 17.3-inch RTX 5090 configurations under Hasee/Shenzhou GX10R9, Machenike/Machinist Star 17 Ultra and AORUS 17X names. They are **CONFLICT**, not candidates proven fit for purchase:

- The exact Hasee `GX10R9` name is independently documented as an older i9-13900HX/RTX 4080 chassis. No current official Hasee SKU page proving an Ultra 9/RTX 5090 refresh was found.
- GIGABYTE's official 17X page remains a 2024 RTX 40-series family and its current official high-end store exposes Master 16/18 rather than a 2026 17X. The 2026 RTX 5090 17X claim therefore conflicts with primary catalog evidence.
- No current official Machenike/Machinist product page, European distributor, Belgian keyboard, EU warranty route or exact three-year cooling-defect coverage was established for the claimed Star 17 Ultra.

Even if a Chinese-only SKU is later proven real, it cannot enter the shortlist without a trustworthy EU transaction, suitable physical keyboard, normal firmware/update route, independent exact-chassis cooling/stability review and three-year Belgian repair path. Import price alone cannot repair these missing gates.
