## Provisional conclusion

There is no clean winner today. MSI 5080 leads purchase readiness and raw value, not platform luxury. Lenovo is the best measured mainstream total chassis but is correctly blocked by firmware uncertainty. ASUS and Acer are the most relevant strict-2026 alternatives. **If the buyer permits a deliberate 2025 exception, the currently orderable XMG NEO 16 E25 becomes one of the strongest direct candidates rather than a watchlist item:** it combines the 175W RTX 5080, matte Mini-LED, aluminium chassis, large glass touchpad and strong reviewed cooling that the MSI lacks. It remains blocked from a final recommendation only by the hard model-year rule and the need to capture the exact Belgian keyboard/OS/warranty cart. The separate 290HX Plus XMG 2026 refresh remains unlaunched. Dell remains the rational fallback until another platform matches its support confidence while materially improving performance and portability.
