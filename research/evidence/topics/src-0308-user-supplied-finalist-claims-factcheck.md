# Factcheck — supplied finalist claims

The supplied prose is not treated as a written manufacturer confirmation. Most sections are secondary syntheses whose citations sometimes point to a family page, another regional SKU or an unrelated product. Each claim is evaluated separately.

## Accepted and recorded

1. **Buyer decision:** exact XMG NEO 16 E25 configuration `10302800` receives a deliberate model-year-2025 exception. This does not reopen other 2025 laptops.
2. **XMG service:** the exact configuration PDF proves 36 months warranty plus Quick Repair throughout the full warranty period. XMG's official service page confirms that Quick Repair is a 48-hour in-house service, Monday–Friday; the warranty card maps full 36-month Quick Repair to the Platinum service level. Transport time is outside the in-house window. Battery wear coverage remains limited separately.
3. **XMG fulfilment:** XMG officially assembles/configures in Leipzig and performs quality checks. The exact 2–3-week estimate is retained as `USER-REPORTED`; the live product page independently displayed an additional 10–14-day delivery time, but does not prove the narrative about final warehouse-batch allocation.
4. **MSI 5080 ports:** MSI's official B2WX family sheet specifies 2× Thunderbolt 4 for both B2WI and B2WJ. This is the correct conservative purchase assumption. An exact Belgian retailer feed still says TB5, so the metadata discrepancy remains documented but is not a functional blocker because TB4 satisfies the requirement.
5. **BTO selectable implementation:** the live selectable 16X1590 Plus+ entry already proves 115W + 25W Dynamic Boost (140W) and a 230W adapter. The broader family table's 175W/330W text remains contradictory; the supplied prose is not a separate BTO letter.
6. **Medion:** exact German `MD600089` is QWERTZ-only. The supplied Galaxus link is a RAM module, not a Belgian laptop transaction; delivery and equivalent third-year repair coverage remain unproven.
7. **Dell keyboard:** exact `2H3VJ` is AZERTY, with exact reseller evidence further identifying AZERTY BE. Exact RTX Pro 3000 TGP remains UNKNOWN.

## Not accepted as exact proof

### XMG Windows licence

Retracted for configuration `10302800`. Its official PDF says `Operating system: Not selected`. XMG's own unboxing guide says a no-Windows order arrives with an empty SSD. No OEM key/UEFI injection may be assumed. A legitimate educational, retail or separately selected OEM route must be identified and costed if Windows is required.

### Lenovo `83F500UAMB` and `83F500SKMB`

The supplied commercial ladder is incompatible with exact PSREF. Exact Belgian PSREF records identify both current MTMs as 290HX Plus, RTX 5080 16GB/175W, 32GB and 1TB. Claims that SKMB is a 275HX sub-top and UAMB a 5090/64GB top model rely on other MTMs/regions and are rejected. The €3799–€4650 numbers are estimates, not live Belgian exact-SKU transactions; third-year €35–€100 figures are also generic, not exact upgrade quotes. Price, stock, SKU difference and third-year total remain UNKNOWN, while the firmware conflict remains.

### MSI Raider 5090 `B2WJ-039BE`

Conflict remains. The Workspace Company lists 290HX Plus and OLED, while MediaMarkt's exact live Belgian listing says 275HX; several other exact-code retailers agree with 275HX. MSI's official B2WJ family page allows multiple CPU and display options and warns that regional configurations differ. No MSI response tied to exact EAN `4711377434980` was supplied. Panel is strongly likely OLED from several exact records, but CPU and panel remain `CONFLICT` under the repository gate.

### ASUS 2026 Belgian transaction

The global ASUS 2026 family page proves 290HX Plus and RTX 5080/P3 possibilities, not the claimed Belgian 32GB/2TB SKU or €3499 transaction. The keyboard-part page proves that a Belgian keyboard part exists, not that a qualifying Belgian retail configuration is stocked. Exact Belgian SKU, price, stock and support remain UNKNOWN.

### Acer `PHN16-I71-956X`

The cited `956X` route is Dutch, not proven Belgian AZERTY. The 150W figure comes from a US `NH.U5TAA.001` SKU and cannot be transferred to `NH.U46EH.006`. The supplied IPS description also conflicts with previously mapped I71 family/OLED data. Exact Belgian SKU, panel, keyboard, TGP, price, support and exact review remain UNKNOWN.

## Consequence

Only XMG's buyer exception/service details, Dell's AZERTY keyboard and the already-known conservative MSI/BTO/Medion fields change active records. No invented Lenovo/ASUS/Acer price or MSI-5090 identity is promoted.
