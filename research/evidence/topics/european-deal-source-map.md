# European laptop deal-source map

Last checked: 2026-08-12

## Recommended search order

1. **Manufacturer stores and configurators** — exact regional SKU, promotions and support.
2. **Tweakers Pricewatch** — strongest first pass for Belgian and Dutch retail.
3. **Geizhals EU** — deepest hardware filters and broad German/Austrian/Polish discovery.
4. **Idealo Germany** — German seller comparison and price-history cross-check.
5. **Deal communities** — Mydealz (DE), Dealabs (FR) and Promojagers (BE) for coupon or flash-sale discovery only.
6. **Komparzon** — Amazon cross-country comparison when the exact regional ASIN/SKU can be isolated.

## Non-European source rule

BestLaptop.Deals currently exposes US dollar offers. Its editorial reviews and model discovery may be useful, but its prices can never enter Belgian budget calculations.

## Transaction gate

No aggregator result is buyable evidence by itself. Open the seller page and prove exact SKU, keyboard, VAT-inclusive total, Belgian delivery, stock, return path and support. German QWERTZ and Dutch QWERTY are separate configurations from Belgian AZERTY.
