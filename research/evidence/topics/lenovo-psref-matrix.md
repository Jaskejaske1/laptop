## Durable conclusion

Lenovo currently presents two recurring trade-offs for A: bright matte touch IPS is normally 100% sRGB, while full-P3 is normally OLED and often non-touch. P14s Gen 6 is the exception with touch/P3 OLED, but its top HX370/890M configuration is not the OLED branch. For B, current Legion PSREF families provide no touch option; touch remains a bonus that cannot presently be satisfied without abandoning core B requirements.
