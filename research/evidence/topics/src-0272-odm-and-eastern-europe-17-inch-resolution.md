Clevo's official current 17.3-inch V370 family is limited to RTX 5070/5060, while its RTX 5080/5090 X580 is 18 inches. Dream Machines' own model codes independently reproduce the break: `RG5060/5070-17` exist, but every 5080/5090 entry is `-16` or `-18`. Hyperbook's current 5080 is GTR Ultra 18; V17 Liquid belongs to the previous RTX 4080/4090 generation. This makes the shortage a chassis-supply result, not merely incomplete Belgian retail indexing.
