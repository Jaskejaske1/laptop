## Dell platform advisories — PROVEN existence, prevalence UNKNOWN

- Dell documents internal-display black/distortion after repeated external-monitor hotplug in discrete mode; NVIDIA driver 32.0.15.7290 A01 or later resolves it.
- Dell documents temporary external-monitor hotplug freezes in discrete mode; NVIDIA driver 32.0.15.9202 A01 or later resolves it.
- Dell documents a GPU-switching BSOD branch and provides a Dell NVIDIA-driver resolution.
- BIOS 2.4.1 may falsely report Intel discrete TB4/TB5 and BIOS update failure even though functionality remains intact. Dell promised BIOS 2.6.0 for 2026-08-11 subject to change, but no public 2.6.0 package was verified on 2026-08-13; official search still exposes 2.4.1 while later 2.5.0 evidence is secondary.
