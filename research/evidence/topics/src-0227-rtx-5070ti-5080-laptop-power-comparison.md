# RTX 5070 Ti versus RTX 5080 Laptop — SRC-0227

## Source

https://www.notebookcheck.net/GeForce-RTX-5070-Ti-Laptop-vs-RTX-5070-Laptop-The-successors-to-the-RTX-4070-Laptop-in-review.1006203.0.html

## What it proves

At a controlled 140W, Notebookcheck measured 17,400 Time Spy Graphics points for RTX 5070 Ti versus 19,696 for RTX 5080, and 82.6 versus 93.2 fps in Cyberpunk 2077 Phantom Liberty QHD/Ultra. The 5070 Ti therefore trails by roughly 11–13% in these measurements rather than falling into a different practical performance class. RTX 5080 retains the important 16GB-versus-12GB VRAM advantage. Exact laptops must still be judged through chassis-level sustained tests.
