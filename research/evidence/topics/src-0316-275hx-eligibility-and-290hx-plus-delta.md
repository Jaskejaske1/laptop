# 275HX eligibility and 290HX Plus delta — SRC-0316

## Sources

- https://download.intel.com/newsroom/news-bytes/core-ultra/Intel_Core_Ultra_200HX_Plus_press_deck.pdf
- https://www.ultrabookreview.com/72307-asus-rog-strix-g16-g615lw-review/

## What they prove

- Core Ultra 9 275HX and 290HX Plus both expose 24 cores / 24 threads as 8P+16E. Intel lists 5.4GHz maximum for 275HX and 5.5GHz for 290HX Plus.
- Intel's own controlled 290HX Plus comparison is against 285HX rather than 275HX. It claims about 3% multi-thread, 3% Premiere/Lightroom, 4% Blender, 5% After Effects/Photoshop and up to 7% single-thread gains. Therefore a precise 290HX-Plus-versus-275HX percentage remains UNKNOWN, but the public evidence supports a refresh-scale rather than class-changing difference.
- A reviewed Strix G16 implementation sustains very high CPU package power and is described as outperforming older 14900HX/13980HX systems. Chassis power policy and cooling can outweigh the CPU suffix.

## Decision relevance

275HX is accepted as a high-end HX processor. Do not reject an otherwise stronger platform merely to obtain 290HX Plus. The Plus part remains a tie-break preference, not a gate.
