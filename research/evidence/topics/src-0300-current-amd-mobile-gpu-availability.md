# Current AMD mobile GPU availability — SRC-0300

- AMD GPUs remain fully eligible under the buyer's platform doctrine.
- AMD's current official gaming-laptop page still presents Radeon RX 7000-series discrete laptop GPUs, while its RX 9000-series product and launch pages describe desktop cards. No current RX 9000-series high-end mobile dGPU or current EU 16–17-inch laptop transaction capable of challenging the Dell baseline was established in this pass.
- Ryzen AI Max platforms with Radeon 8000-series integrated graphics are technically interesting, but do not provide the discrete-GPU performance tier required for Laptop B's heavy rendering, video, gaming and local-AI role.
- Therefore the active absence of AMD finalists is a market-availability and performance result, not a brand exclusion. Reopen immediately when AMD or an OEM publishes a current high-end mobile Radeon platform with an exact EU route and qualifying reviews.
