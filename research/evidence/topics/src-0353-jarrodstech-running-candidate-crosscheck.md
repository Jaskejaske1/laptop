# Jarrod'sTech crosscheck of the Laptop B field — SRC-0353

## Scope rule

Jarrod's review verdict may be transferred only to the same current chassis generation. His RTX 5090 review unit does not prove the performance of a Belgian RTX 5080 SKU, and a 2024 NEO review does not validate the 2025 E25. Absence of a located exact review means `UNKNOWN`, not a negative verdict.

## Selection consequence

Jarrod's evidence creates a well-supported top group of Legion Pro 7i, SCAR 16 and regular Strix G16, but it does not by itself select the winner. For this buyer, the SCAR combines the strongest Jarrod-backed premium display/input/repair story with a materially better Belgian price/support package than the current Legion. The Legion remains the strongest raw-performance endorsement but carries a weaker touchpad/value fit. The regular Strix remains the value-performance option. MSI Raider and XMG NEO remain legitimate contenders, but they cannot claim Jarrod-backed exact-platform validation.
