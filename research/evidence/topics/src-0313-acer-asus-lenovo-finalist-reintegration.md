# Acer is not yet a too-good-to-be-true winner

The exact Dutch Acer hardware sheet is internally credible, but its €3,999 sticker is not a complete Belgian/support-adjusted price and is not anomalously low beside MSI's €3,861.62 complete three-year route or XMG's €4,123 three-year configuration.

Acer's official PHN16-I71 launch specification confirms a cost-tier distinction that the retailer sheet does not show: cooling combines one fifth-generation AeroBlade 3D fan with a separate **plastic fan**, liquid-metal thermal compound and a vector heat pipe. The platform also uses Wi-Fi 6E, two speakers, one TB4 port, a 64GB stated RAM ceiling and a 1TB factory SSD in the exact NL offer. None is a hard failure, but together they prevent treating the attractive CPU/GPU/display sheet as proof of a premium workstation-like implementation.

A fresh exact-model search still found no professional review of the full-size 2026 `PHN16-I71`. Reviews or impressions of the thin `PHN16S-I71` and previous PHN16 generations cannot establish its touchpad, chassis rigidity, sustained power, fan character, battery or DPC.

# ASUS and Lenovo remain active blocked leads

- Exact French ASUS Strix G16 2026 `G615LW-TS357W` remains technically qualifying: 290HX Plus, 175W RTX 5080, matte P3 display, two SO-DIMM, two M.2, 2x TB5, RJ45 and three-year warranty for €3,999.99. It is not a Belgian transaction, carries French AZERTY, ASUS France does not ship it to Belgium and no independent 2026 Strix chassis review was found. The older 275HX review cannot prove the redesigned 2026 behavior.
- Exact Belgian Lenovo Legion Pro 7i `83F500UAMB` and `83F500SKMB` remain technically among the strongest: Belgian AZERTY, aluminium top/bottom, 175W RTX 5080, strong same-chassis cooling and acceptable battery. They are blocked by the unresolved current Q7CN78WW BIOS conflict, absent live Belgian price/stock and unpriced third year. The Mylar 120x75mm touchpad and glossy OLED are also weaker preference fits than XMG's glass pad and matte Mini-LED.

No status was changed: Acer remains `verifying`, ASUS remains a region-blocked verifying lead and Lenovo remains `CONFLICT`. A Lenovo upstream BIOS resolution or a Belgian 2026 ASUS transaction would return either directly to the top comparison.
