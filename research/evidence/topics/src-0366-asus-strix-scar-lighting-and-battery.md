# ASUS lighting and battery — SRC-0366

`AniMe Vision` is the SCAR's 810-white-LED matrix behind perforations in the outside display lid. It can show preset animations, text and imported GIFs. It faces away from the user and is cosmetic. The SCAR also has full-surround chassis lighting and per-key RGB: every keyboard key can receive an individual colour/effect. The ordinary Strix uses four keyboard lighting zones and fewer decorative chassis LEDs. None changes typing, cooling or performance.

Both tested 2025 machines use 90Wh batteries. Exact Intel/RTX5080 Strix testing at 120 nits/60Hz produced about 6–7 hours text or YouTube, 5–6 hours Netflix HDR and 4–5 hours browsing with RGB off. Tom's Hardware measured the exact RTX5080 SCAR for 6h30 in its 150-nit mixed web/video/OpenGL protocol. These are different protocols and do not prove a universal fixed gap. The IPS Strix is the more efficient screen route in matched long-form observations, while Mini-LED zone mode plus AniMe/RGB can reduce SCAR runtime. Both remain desktop replacements rather than all-day mobile systems.
