## RTX 5090 review battery evidence

- Notebookcheck's exact-current `B2WJ` 290HX Plus/RTX5090/OLED chassis measured 7h52 Wi-Fi from 91.8Wh. Its SCAR G635LX RTX5090 test measured 6h28 under the same WLAN framework, a 1h24 or approximately 22% MSI advantage.
- Tom's Hardware measured 8h34 in its mixed 150-nit Wi-Fi test, versus 6h30 for the compared ASUS. This is independent confirmation within a second protocol.
- PCWorld measured a little over eight hours offline video and observed approximately five to six hours of real browsing/article drafting. It specifically attributes the result to reliable dGPU disabling on battery.
- TechRadar measured 8h11 in its movie test. Video and mixed tests are not interchangeable, but all four results support `good for a full-power gaming laptop`, not merely a single anomalous benchmark.

## Platform interpretation

- The MSI's 5090 is officially up to 175W and the platform advertises 300W combined CPU/GPU power. Professional exact-current review coverage confirms very high sustained performance.
- Its mobility advantage does not erase the known ownership compromises: mostly plastic deck/base, professionally conflicting 130x83mm touchpad behavior, keyboard-layout criticism, rebooting discrete-mode switch and no internal G-SYNC/Advanced Optimus on the OLED path.
- Conversely, no recurring current-chassis cooling, driver or firmware failure pattern has been established. MSHybrid/dGPU sleep behavior is better evidenced than on several rivals.
