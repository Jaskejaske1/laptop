# PCSpecialist Recoil 16 — controlled deep audit

Hardwareluxx independently tested the 9955HX3D/RTX 5080/Mini-LED platform. The predominantly aluminium chassis was rigid and well finished, hinges stable, keyboard suitable for fast typing and the 123x78mm glass clickpad accurate with good glide/palm rejection. KitGuru's separate current-chassis 5070 Ti unit found the pad's tracking, taps and clicks inconsistent, so input execution remains `CONFLICT` rather than averaged away.

Cooling evidence is unusually strong for the price. Hardwareluxx found no thermally caused performance reduction, 76C sustained CPU/85C peak, about 75C GPU, 40.5C maximum exterior, 34.7dB(A) idle, 45.3dB(A) gaming and 49.1dB(A) maximum. Balanced held the GPU at 150W; Turbo and especially the optional external liquid loop enable higher sustained platform power. Battery measured 376 minutes light load, 282 minutes mixed partial load and 93 minutes full load.

The Mini-LED review panel reached almost 800cd/m2 in SDR, covered sRGB and DCI-P3 fully and was well calibrated. PCSpecialist rates the option to 1250 nits/HDR. The platform supplies two M.2, two SO-DIMM, 99Wh and full 175W maximum GPU configuration.

The Intel configuration is the preferred purchase shape because TB4 satisfies the fast-port requirement; the AMD configuration has only USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-C and is not credited with USB4. Same-chassis construction/input observations may transfer as `LIKELY`, while exact Intel sustained behavior, DPC and driver/firmware robustness remain `UNKNOWN` absent an exact professional test.

The support model blocks promotion. Gold is materially weaker than Dell ProSupport and fails the stored third-year parts/collection requirement. Platinum would close the formal duration gap but must first be made selectable or quoted in writing; it would still remain depot service rather than Dell's business support experience.
