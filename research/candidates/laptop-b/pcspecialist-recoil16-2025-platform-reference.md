# PCSpecialist Recoil 16 platform reference

- **GPU:** configurable GeForce RTX 5070 Ti, RTX 5080 16GB or RTX 5090; reviewed unit used RTX 5080 16GB.
- **Official TGP:** 150W + 25W Dynamic Boost for RTX 5080/5090 on the reviewed/configured platform.
- **Sustained GPU:** PROVEN for reviewed AMD/RTX 5080 chassis: balanced mode held 150W; full platform power required Turbo and benefited from external liquid cooling.
- **RAM / upgrade:** two DDR5 SODIMM slots, configurable to 96GB.
- **M.2:** two M.2 2280 slots, one PCIe 5.0 x4 and one PCIe 4.0 x4.
- **Thermals / noise:** PROVEN reviewed chassis: no thermal performance drop, GPU about 75°C; 34.7dBA idle, 45.3dBA gaming, 49.1dBA maximum.
- **DPC:** UNKNOWN.

This independently reviewed aluminium platform demonstrates that a non-mainstream gaming integrator can deliver many workstation-like qualities being sought: rigid chassis, stable hinges, a 123x78mm glass touchpad, strong thermals, restrained gaming noise, 99Wh and high expansion. Touchpad execution is nevertheless `CONFLICT`: Hardwareluxx found good glide, precision, click and palm rejection, while KitGuru found tracking, clicking and tapping frustratingly inconsistent on the current Recoil/Uniwill chassis.

The exact Belgian transaction layer is now resolved. Intel Recoil III with 275HX, 175W-rated RTX 5080, Mini-LED P3, 32GB, Samsung 990 Pro 1TB, Belgian keyboard and no OS was **€3315 incl. VAT/delivery** on 2026-08-14. The exact professionally reviewed AMD 9955HX3D route was **€3041**, or €3201 with the optional liquid cooler, but it exposes only 10Gbps USB-C rather than the required/preferred USB4; Intel is therefore the better port fit. The new 2026 Recoil IV was €3752 with 290HX Plus/RTX 5090/32GB/1TB, but has no exact professional review yet and only Gigabit Ethernet.

The purchase blocker is support rather than price or core hardware. All three live configurators expose only included Gold coverage: three years labor but **two years parts and two years collection**. PCSpecialist's warranty page describes Platinum with three years parts/labor/collection, but Platinum was not selectable and no price was exposed. Until PCSpecialist supplies a written Platinum route, cooling hardware in year three is not fully covered. The platform remains `verifying`, not a finalist.
