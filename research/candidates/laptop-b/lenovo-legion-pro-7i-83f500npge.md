## Decision

Technically one of the strongest reference results, but the exact SKU is buyer-excluded because its German QWERTZ keyboard is unacceptable. Isolated owner reports mention NVMe/BSOD and audio trouble under BIOS Q7CN78WW, but no repeated independent same-fault pattern or professional reproduction is established; under the research protocol these remain acceptance-test signals rather than a platform defect. Keep the €3799/three-year-support configuration only as a value reference. The Legion family may re-enter through a separately verified Belgian AZERTY or genuine US International exact SKU; do not transfer this German price or support package to it.
