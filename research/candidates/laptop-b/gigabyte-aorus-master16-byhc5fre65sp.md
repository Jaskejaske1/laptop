# GIGABYTE AORUS Master 16 BYHC5FRE65SP

- **Live transaction:** Fnac Belgium Marketplace, sold by 1foDiscount, €4265.22 plus €12.99 delivery = **€4278.21**. It was orderable on 14 August 2026 with a stated 26 August–7 September delivery window.
- **Regional isolation:** this is explicitly **French AZERTY**, not Belgian AZERTY. No exact Belgian-AZERTY or proven US-International AM6H transaction was found.
- **CPU:** Core Ultra 9 275HX.
- **GPU:** RTX 5080 Laptop GPU with 16GB GDDR7.
- **Official TGP:** **175W** maximum graphics power with Dynamic Boost, proven by the official BYH family specification and the exact Belgian commerce record.
- **Sustained GPU:** full-power chassis behavior is PROVEN across exact RTX-5080 professional testing and same-chassis stress loops; 175W is the permitted ceiling rather than a promised constant draw in every mixed CPU/GPU workload.
- **RAM / upgrade:** 32GB as 2x16GB DDR5; two SO-DIMM sockets and 64GB official maximum.
- **M.2:** 2TB installed; two slots, one Gen5 and one Gen4.
- **Display:** glossy 2560x1600 240Hz OLED with G-SYNC and Advanced Optimus. Gigabyte specifies 100% DCI-P3/500-nit peak. Exact/same-panel measurements found 99.2% P3 with DeltaE 1.01 and 437-nit full-screen SDR/635-nit HDR APL25; Tom's exact 5080 unit returned a lower 81% P3 result, so panel/method variance remains visible rather than erased.
- **I/O:** separate 330W DC-in, one TB5, one TB4, two 10Gbps USB-A, HDMI 2.1, 1GbE RJ45, UHS-II microSD and audio. Both USB-C ports accept up to 100W PD for light use; full performance requires the barrel-style DC input.
- **Thermals / noise:** the two-fan vapor-chamber design is genuinely capable. Exact RTX-5080 testing found no thermal throttling, approximately 96C CPU and 76C GPU. Same-chassis testing measured fans off/23dB(A) at idle and roughly 59.75dB(A) under maximum gaming load. The quiet light-load behavior fits the buyer; maximum noise is acceptable for renders.
- **Performance:** materially faster GPU/VRAM class than Dell `2H3VJ`. The 275HX remains powerful but professional exact-5080 testing found its multicore implementation somewhat power-limited compared with the fastest 275HX laptops.
- **Battery:** 99Wh. Professional protocols measured 2h15 in PCMark Modern Office at maximum brightness, about four hours moderate use, 5h02 mixed light use and 6h44 WLAN on the same chassis. It is mobile enough for occasional light work but not a battery leader.
- **Construction:** mostly plastic, stable in torsion but with a somewhat loose/bouncy lid and reports of deck flex. Several professional reviewers independently say it feels below its flagship price.
- **Keyboard/touchpad:** 1.7mm three-zone RGB keyboard; reviews range from comfortable to small/soft with low-contrast legends. The 133x90mm mechanical touchpad is large and usually reviewed as smooth/responsive, but one professional unit repeatedly stuck on left click. Marked `CONFLICT` rather than universally poor.
- **Firmware/software:** GiMate provides automatic driver/BIOS updating and mode control. Notebookcheck says later BIOS/GiMate updates fixed initial hitching and lag, but still found optimization room. Tom's Hardware had one Red Dead Redemption 2 benchmark crash. This does **not** prove chronic platform instability, but integration is less polished than ASUS or Dell.
- **DPC:** same AM6H chassis with RTX 5090 measured 1637.7 microseconds and 10 dropped 4K60 frames. This is a LIKELY chassis warning; exact `BYHC5FRE65SP` DPC remains UNKNOWN.
- **Repair/support:** RAM, both SSDs, fans and screwed battery are accessible, though the bottom RGB cable requires care; French repairability index is 6.1/10. The live seller provides two years. Generic AORUS registration adds three months, not the required full third year, and Fnac exposes no priced qualifying three-year cooling-defect package.

## Dell-relative decision

This is a legitimate high-performance alternative, not a paper reject. It beats Dell on raw GPU class, VRAM, size and TB5/TB4 flexibility, and its light-load fan behavior is excellent. At the current Belgian-marketplace total it saves only €165.96 before adding a missing third support year, while losing Belgian AZERTY, ProSupport, premium construction and touchpad certainty. It therefore stays `watchlist`, below SCAR, unless a roughly €3200–€3500 deliverable route with acceptable keyboard and three-year repair coverage appears.
