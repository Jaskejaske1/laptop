# MSI Vector 17 HX AI A2XW

Technically compelling 17-inch QHD+ 240Hz full-P3 IPS family with 175W RTX 5090 24GB, Core Ultra 9 275HX, two Thunderbolt 5 ports and strong sustained GPU cooling. The buyer explicitly granted this Vector a narrow exception to the 2026 model-year rule. It nevertheless remains rejected under the stricter MSI platform gate: an exact-chassis professional review encountered up to 60W idle draw and excessive noise that BIOS/MSI Center updates did not fix; a clean Windows reinstall was required. The same review also found the automatic AI profile dropped more than 1,000 Cinebench R15 points under sustained CPU load and required the 60.35dB Extreme profile for stable maximum CPU output.

- **GPU:** up to RTX 5090 Laptop 24GB.
- **Official TGP:** 175W maximum; 250W combined platform.
- **Sustained GPU:** PROVEN at 172W average for one RTX 5090 review unit over one hour; a separate RTX 5080 review measured 160W in Cyberpunk despite a 175W official ceiling.
- **RAM / upgrade:** two slots, family maximum 96GB.
- **M.2:** family offers two M.2; exact regional mapping not pursued after rejection.
- **Thermals / noise:** cooling capacity is strong (RTX 5090 review: 79C core/75C VRAM at 172W), but full-power acoustics are poor: 60.35dB in AI/Extreme in Notebookcheck. Balanced was 47.4dB with GPU capped around 130W; Eco Silent was 36.21dB/80W. Exact-chassis idle/software behavior is inconsistent between reviews.
- **DPC:** 1133.8us in Notebookcheck with no dropped 4K60 frames.
- **Build/input:** aluminium lid, plastic deck/base; independently described as very solid without creaking. Large 131x81mm smooth, precise touchpad, though the shared-chassis click mechanism was described as slightly flimsy. Keyboard typing feel is good but the small arrows, shrunken numpad and unconventional layout waste the 17-inch deck.
- **Display:** matte 2560x1600/240Hz IPS, measured 473-nit average, 99.6% P3 and excellent calibrated accuracy, no PWM. Weak 659:1 contrast and elevated black level make it less visually premium than OLED/Mini-LED, while avoiding OLED ageing risk.
- **Battery:** 6h44 Wi-Fi in Notebookcheck; 5h42 video at 180 nits in LaptopMedia. Good for this class, not workstation-level endurance.
- **Repairability:** two SO-DIMMs, two M.2, replaceable Wi-Fi and screwed battery/fans. Component access is good after opening, but Notebookcheck found removing the full lower shell unusually awkward.
