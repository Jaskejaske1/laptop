# GIGABYTE AORUS Master 16 Gen 2 / 6XJM4EEE64SP

- **Generation:** 2026 AM6J chassis.
- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX3D, 16 cores / 32 threads. This is a current 2026 laptop configuration, although the Fire Range CPU itself predates the 2026 chassis.
- **GPU:** RTX 5070 Ti 12GB.
- **Official TGP:** PROVEN at 140W maximum graphics power with Dynamic Boost for the official 6XJ family branch.
- **Sustained GPU:** UNKNOWN for the exact 5070 Ti configuration pending an independent load review.
- **Display:** 2560x1600 240Hz OLED, official 100% DCI-P3 and 1000-nit peak. Sustained SDR brightness and burn-in policy remain to be verified.
- **RAM / upgrade:** exact retailer lead states 32GB; chassis has two SO-DIMMs and supports up to 64GB.
- **M.2:** exact retailer lead states 1TB; chassis has one Gen5 plus one Gen4 M.2 slot.
- **I/O:** RJ45 1GbE, USB4, second USB-C, two USB-A, HDMI 2.1, UHS-II microSD and audio.
- **Battery:** same-current-chassis review measured less than 2.5 hours web and 3h46 video despite 99Wh; LIKELY for the exact SKU, not an exact 5070 Ti measurement.
- **Thermals / noise:** same-current-chassis review found effective sustained cooling but high-pitched fans that ramped under light load and remained objectionable in Quiet mode.
- **DPC:** UNKNOWN; no exact 5070 Ti LatencyMon result established.
- **Offer:** Alternate NL indexed the exact SKU at €3149, but it was not in stock and showed no delivery information.
- **Decision:** rejected from the normal shortlist. Its full-power 5070 Ti sheet is strong, but unavailable stock plus professional same-chassis evidence of extremely short battery life and intrusive light-load fan behavior directly conflicts with the buyer's priorities. This is a chassis-behavior rejection, not an inference that the GPU or cooling capacity is weak.
