# MSI Stealth 16 AI+ B3WI-017BE

## Factchecked assessment

- **Transaction:** supplied Belgian catalog export shows €3213.59, 20+ in stock, Belgian regional SKU and 32GB/1TB. The export does not unambiguously prove charger inclusion or a priced third support year.
- **Model year:** genuine 2026 redesign with Core Ultra 9 386H Panther Lake.
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 Laptop GPU with 16GB GDDR7.
- **Official TGP:** RTX 5080 16GB at **125W maximum including Dynamic Boost**. This is not the requested 175W implementation.
- **Sustained GPU:** exact long-duration board-power logging is UNKNOWN; official ceiling and completed professional performance suite prove it can never be the requested 175W implementation.
- **Performance:** Tweakers' exact B3WI review measured 84.8fps Cyberpunk 1440p Ultra versus 102.8fps for its 175W Raider comparison. The H-class 386H also trails HX CPUs in long CPU-heavy renders.
- **RAM / upgrade:** factory 32GB; two DDR5 SO-DIMM slots, up to 128GB.
- **M.2:** two M.2 Gen4 slots; one occupied by the factory 1TB drive.
- **Display:** glossy 2560x1600 240Hz OLED, full P3, HDR True Black 600. Tweakers measured only 379–384cd/m² white and good calibration; this fails the buyer's 500-nit glossy/OLED preference.
- **I/O:** family sheet proves 2x TB4 and 2.5GbE/RJ45; exact regional port mapping remains subject to exact sheet confirmation but the chassis meets the functional I/O direction.
- **Inputs/build:** large, smooth touchpad but not haptic; sturdy metal body. Tweakers found visible keyboard-deck flex under typing, which weakens the premium impression.
- **Thermals / noise:** 20.3dBA during light browsing, 33.0dBA PCMark, 52.4dBA Blender and 50.9dBA gaming. Fans remain audible during browsing. Exact internal temperatures were not published in this Tweakers quicktest; thermal-failure evidence is UNKNOWN, while excessive acoustics are PROVEN.
- **Battery:** 90Wh and an excellent 9h30 controlled web result; 2h07 under PCMark with dGPU use. This is substantially more mobile than Raider-class HX systems.
- **DPC:** LatencyMon/DPC remains UNKNOWN; Ableton is outside the active purchase gate.
- **Stability:** the professional test completed its suite and no recurring driver/firmware failure pattern was found. That is positive normal-test evidence, not seven-year enterprise proof.
- **Dell delta:** much lighter and clearly better battery portability, plus large touchpad. It loses decisively in sustained GPU power, HX CPU throughput, glossy brightness, support proof and enterprise platform assurances. It cannot be the workstation B replacement under current priorities.

## Verdict

This is a good 2026 hybrid laptop and a poor match for the specific B role. It is rejected only after exact TGP, review measurements, screen and acoustics were checked—not because it says MSI.
