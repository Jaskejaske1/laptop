# Gigabyte AORUS Master 16 (2025)

## Migrated assessment

- **Price:** the exact French `BYHC5FRE65SP` RTX-5080/32GB/2TB SKU is orderable on Fnac Belgium Marketplace for €4278.21 delivered, but this is not a Belgian-keyboard SKU. A separate exact record preserves that transaction.
- **CPU:** Core Ultra 9 275HX
- **GPU:** RTX 5080 16 GB
- **Official TGP:** 175 W PROVEN review unit
- **Sustained GPU:** full-power class PROVEN
- **RAM / upgrade:** 32 GB review unit; 2 SODIMM
- **M.2:** 2
- **Display / gamut:** 2560x1600 240Hz OLED, wide gamut
- **Thermals / noise:** exact/same-chassis professional evidence shows strong cooling and exceptionally good idle behavior: fans are usually off at idle and measured 23dB(A), but reach about 59.75dB(A) in full Gaming load. Exact RTX-5080 testing found no thermal throttling, about 96C CPU and 76C GPU; CPU multicore performance is somewhat power-limited relative to the fastest 275HX implementations.
- **Battery:** protocol-dependent results range from 2h15 PCMark Modern Office at maximum brightness to 5h02 mixed light use and 6h44 WLAN on the same chassis. This is workable desktop-replacement mobility, not SCAR-class integration or all-day endurance.
- **DPC:** same-chassis 5090 review measured 1637.7 microseconds with 10 dropped 4K60 frames; LIKELY chassis warning, not exact BYH proof.
- **Touchpad:** large 133x90mm mechanical pad. Four professional reviews call it smooth/responsive or excellent, while Notebookcheck's unit repeatedly stuck on left click. Execution is therefore `CONFLICT`, not universally bad and not safely premium.
- **Notes:** Excellent I/O including separate DC-in, TB5, TB4, RJ45 and UHS-II microSD; Ethernet is only 1GbE. The platform is fast and quiet at light load, but its mostly-plastic shell, conflicting touchpad execution, very loud maximum profile, French keyboard route and missing three-year repair package keep it below the active SCAR shortlist.
