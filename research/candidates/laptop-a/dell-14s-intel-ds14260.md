# Dell 14S Intel / DS14260

## Migrated assessment

- **Price:** €1298.80 exact BE base 16/512
- **CPU:** Core Ultra 5 332 base; Panther Lake options
- **RAM:** exact base 1x16 DDR5; higher 32GB configurations exist
- **SSD:** 512GB base; 1TB higher configurations
- **Display / gamut:** exact base LCD is narrow-gamut 45% NTSC; reviewed glossy OLED measured 269-nit SDR and therefore fails glossy 500-nit rule
- **Battery evidence:** 20h41 Wi-Fi at 150 nit in one review; about 10h observed office use and 21h59 PCMark in another
- **Touchpad / keyboard:** reviews: comfortable responsive keyboard and good smooth/clicky touchpad
- **Key unknowns:** none material after display rejection
- **Notes:** Excellent battery, aluminium build and 2xTB4+2xA, but no RJ45 and neither the base LCD nor reviewed OLED meets the revised display rule. Rejection is not based on 16GB alone.
