## CPU naming boundary

- `Core Ultra 7 270K Plus` is a desktop Core Ultra 200S Plus/Arrow Lake Refresh processor; `K` is not a laptop suffix.
- `Core Ultra 7 270HX Plus` is the distinct mobile high-performance part, with `Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus` above it. Current 2026 ASUS Strix machines found use 290HX Plus; Dell Alienware exposes 270HX Plus.
- `Core Ultra 9 275HX` is the 2025 mobile HX part in the parked Strix/SCAR systems. Panther Lake mobile parts use 300-series names such as `Core Ultra 9 386H`; that efficient H-class chip is not equivalent to an HX workstation CPU.
